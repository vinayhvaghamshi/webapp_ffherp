FFH|ERP team & leadership — public/team/
=========================================

team.csv        <- edit this to change who appears on the About page's
                   Leadership cards, and what each card says.
*.jpeg / *.png  <- the headshots referenced by the 'photo' column.

To change a name, title, bio or photo order
  Edit team.csv. One row per person, in the order they should appear.

To add or remove a person
  Add or delete a row.

To give someone a photograph
  Drop a square headshot here (600x600 or larger) and put its filename in the
  'photo' column, e.g.  gopal.jpeg
  With no photo the card shows their initials — never a stock face for a real
  person. The 'avatar' column is only for temporary placeholders and should be
  blanked out once a real photo exists.
